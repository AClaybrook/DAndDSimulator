# Act 1 item kits

Sets of Act 1 items that work together and move between builds. Each kit lists what triggers it, the core pieces by slot (where they're found), and the slots it competes for.
Locations are from bg3.wiki via the planner's item data; the map page (/map) shows each one. ★ = the piece the kit is built around.

## Damage kits (triggered by a damage type or action)

### Thunder / Reverberation
Trigger: thunder damage every turn (Booming Blade, Thunderwave, Thunderous Smite). Also radiant and lightning for the gloves.
| Slot | Item | Where | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gloves ★ | Gloves of Belligerent Skies | Crèche Y'llek | Thunder, lightning or radiant damage: 2 turns of Reverberation |
| Boots ★ | Boots of Stormy Clamour | Ebonlake Grotto | Inflicting any condition (Reverberation counts): 2 more turns |
| Amulet | Necklace of Elemental Augmentation | Crèche Y'llek | Elemental cantrip damage (Booming Blade's thunder) + spellcasting modifier |
| Ring | Ring of Elemental Infusion | Crèche Y'llek | After an elemental spell/cantrip, the next weapon hit +1d4 |
| Weapon | Cacophony (staff) | Rosymorn Monastery Trail | Thunderous Smite, +1d4 thunder |
| Ring | Ring of Absolute Force | Grymforge | Thunderwave per short rest |

5 stacks of Reverberation: 1d4 thunder and a CON save (effectively DC 15) or Prone, so melee allies get advantage. Each stack is −1 to STR/DEX/CON saves, which helps Hold Person, Command and Thunderwave land.
Fits: Bladesinger/Godblade, Hexblade, Eldritch Knight, Tempest Cleric, Swords Bard (Booming Blade). Competes: gloves (Bracers of Defence), boots, amulet.

## Party combinations that feed each other
- **Thunder kit + Hold Person caster:** Reverberation lowers STR/DEX saves and Prone gives melee advantage. Pair it with a Paralysed, Bless or Sing user.
- **Lightning kit + Wet:** Create Water (Rain Dancer staff from The Hollow, or a Tempest Cleric) makes enemies vulnerable to lightning and cold.
- **Healer kit + front line:** Hellrider's Pride and The Whispering Promise turn heals into resistance and +1d4 to attacks and saves.
- **Psychic weapons + Resonance Stone** (from late Act 2).
